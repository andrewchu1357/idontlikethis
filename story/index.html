Our story

We started this site in a coffee shop during a slow Friday afternoon, when we got tired of polished marketing language and wanted something more honest.

From the beginning, the idea was simple: publish real opinions, useful insights, and no-nonsense updates. No "revolutionizing", no "disrupting", no "empowering communities at scale". Just a website that says what it means — even when what it means is "we don't like this".

Today, we keep the same spirit. Every page, every word, and every feature is meant to feel direct, easy to understand, and refreshingly genuine. If we ever add a newsletter popup, you have permission to close the tab forever.
